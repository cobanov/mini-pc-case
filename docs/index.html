Mini PC Case

H310M-K · GTX 1050 Ti · ATX PSU · 176 × 204 × 265 mm · 9.7 L

View

Qualitative, not CFD: blue is cool, red is warmed air. Bottom fan → GPU → cooler → top fan. The PSU takes its own air through the side grille and exhausts at the rear.

Color palettes

A palette sets the three groups below: body, panels, feet. The swatch in a group header repaints that group; the swatch next to a part changes it alone. Pick a panel's variant from its menu; the upper body comes in three fronts.
